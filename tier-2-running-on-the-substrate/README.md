# Tier 2: Running on the Substrate

*The goal of Tier 2 is to understand how a platform runs on hardware it does not own.*

A platform needs no orchestrator of its own: it picks one from the substrate market and pays transport out of the trade, on hardware the substrate layer obtains and records (line 11).
