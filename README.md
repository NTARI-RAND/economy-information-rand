# economy-information-rand

Research & Development on the Economy & Information Layer of the Janus Facing Architecture.

Where JFA meets the world: one platform per kind of exchange, run on the substrate, recorded in the Record layer, under the covenant. Leaving is cheap by design.

Research is discussed in #rand-economy-information and voted on in #federation-economy-information in the NTARI Slack workspace.

## Structure

| Tier | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Tier 1: Platform Protocols](tier-1-platform-protocols/) | Understand the exchange each platform exists for, and write a protocol that fits it |
| [Tier 2: Running on the Substrate](tier-2-running-on-the-substrate/) | Understand how a platform runs on hardware it does not own |
| [Tier 3: Platform Frontends](tier-3-platform-frontends/) | Implement interfaces that people can customize and leave without losing what they built |

## How we research

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your file to the correct tier folder.
3. Create a pull request with a [DCO signature](https://github.com/apps/dco).
4. Wait for peer review.

#federation-economy-information has 90 days to deliberate your publication, during which you defend your claims and make the changes reviewers ask for. Follow the [Research & Development Submission Guide](https://ntari.slack.com/docs/T0978JCFFSL/F0BTB5N93A9): the method, the ethics check and the submission template.

Merged research is published in #federation-volunteers, on [www.ntari.org](https://www.ntari.org) and affiliated social media channels, with the author(s) credited.
